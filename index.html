About

Parking polygons/nodes are fetched live from the Overpass API (amenity=parking) within the Riga center bounding box. For each parking feature the centroid is used as its access point.

The grid is 5 m square cells covering ~2 × 2 km around Doma laukums. For each cell we compute the Haversine distance to the closest parking centroid and multiply by 1.3 to approximate street-walking detour; this is rendered as a coloured canvas overlay.
